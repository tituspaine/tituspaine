# INTEL.TITUSPAINE.COM — Product Directive V2

INTEL is a permanent public intelligence, investigative publishing, evidence, and discussion platform operated by Titus Paine. Investigations are living public records: persistent containers accumulating findings, evidence, documents, sources, updates, timelines, entities, relationships, questions, corrections, and public discussion.

## Non-negotiables

- GitHub repository `tituspaine/tituspaine` is the sole source of truth.
- Cloudflare is the application infrastructure: Workers, D1, R2, DNS/CDN, Turnstile, caching, security, secrets, and available observability.
- `tituspaine.com` remains operational; INTEL is a distinct product at `intel.tituspaine.com`.
- Public reading requires no account. Accounts enable follows, likes, comments, reports, notifications, and personal state.
- Public user comments are immutable after publication. Normal moderation changes visibility, never silently rewrites history.
- Official content, source material, community submissions, comments, questions, allegations, analysis, and corrections must be visibly distinct.
- Provenance, corrections, auditability, permanent URLs, and structured entities are first-class product behavior.
- UI: white background, black typography, blue interactions; mobile-first, dense, restrained, accessible, fast, and editorial rather than SaaS/dashboard-like.

## V1 completion

V1 is complete only after production verification of public investigations, auth/sessions, following, immutable threaded comments, likes, reporting and 10-eligible-report auto-hide, moderation, user dashboard, notifications, evidence/entities, admin publishing, R2 attachments, search, push/PWA, sharing/SEO, security, navigation history, responsive QA, performance, and recovery documentation.

## Publication standard

Official investigative material explicitly distinguishes known/documented fact, sourced material, analysis, questions, third-party allegations, unverified information, and corrections. Credibility and reconstructable history take precedence over dramatic presentation.
